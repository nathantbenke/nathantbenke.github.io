Hey there, I'm Nathan, an XR developer with a master's in Virtual and Augmented Reality from Goldsmiths, University of London, and a bachelor's in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. That mix gave me a strong technical foundation and a specialization in a field I've wanted to work in for a long time.

I build hands-on, real-time 3D and XR applications in Unity with C#, shipping across Meta Quest, HTC VIVE, and Android and iOS tablets. I've had the chance to work for industry leaders: digital-twin visualizations at Sandia National Laboratories, interactive AR content at 3D Systems, and VR training at ARCS Aviation across DoD and U.S. government contracts.

My master's thesis explored real-time graphics remapping, retexturing real-world objects live from RealSense depth streams, and it's now growing into Lucid, one of my NTB Labs projects. Other work has been recognized nationally: BeatBoxVR won the Jury Prize at the Innovate UK Immersive Tech Awards, and I was a finalist and speaker at AWE EU with 4th Frontier, a small XR development group I'm part of.

My most recent project is NTB Labs, the umbrella for everything I build across disciplines, from developer tools to games to music.

NTB Labs is my independent software studio: dev tools, games, and creative work, built in public.

From browser extensions to native apps to real-time 3D. One studio, many disciplines.

Selected Work

Professional · ARCS Aviation

2024 – Present

ARCS Trainer

XR Developer

A VR training platform for complex mechanical procedures.

Problem

Hands-on practice on real aviation equipment is expensive, slow to schedule, and high-consequence. Trainees need repetitions that physical hardware can't provide.

Approach

I lead development of VR training simulations on Meta Quest and HTC VIVE: interactive tools that teach complex mechanical procedures through hands-on practice in full-scale virtual environments. I own the interaction layer (OpenXR / XR Interaction Toolkit) and scene performance for large environments.

Early results

In an initial pilot, a small cohort of maintenance workers who supplemented their existing paper procedures with the VR training showed improved performance and responded strongly positively to XR as a training and learning medium.

Unity · OpenXR · XR Interaction Toolkit · Meta Quest · HTC VIVE
